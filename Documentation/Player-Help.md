# Player Help – DKP Bot

## What you can use

- **`/my_dkp`**  
  See your current DKP balance in a private embed.

- **`/auction_help`**  
  Explains how the auction system works and how bidding works step‑by‑step.

- **`/ping`**  
  Quick connectivity check. Bot replies “Pong” if it’s online.

## During Raids

Most raid control (starting/ending raids, awarding points, auctions, etc.) is handled by Officers/Raid Leaders.  
You mainly need to:

- Use **`/my_dkp`** to check how many points you have.  
- Follow the instructions in **`/auction_help`** for bidding when items are auctioned.
