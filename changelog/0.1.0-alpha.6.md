## Guild Bank

- Added Guild Bank feature with deposit, withdraw, searchable inventory channel, and transaction log channel with auto-sync.
- Deposit modal uses a UserSelect (with autocomplete) for the held-by field; simplified overall modal layout.
- Deposit/withdraw success messages are public (visible to all); dedicated inventory and transactions channels replace ephemeral-only visibility.
- Deleted item slot IDs are reused to maintain compact slot numbering in the inventory.
- Guild bank data is fully cleared by the reset command.

## Raid improvements

- Added public audit message for DKP reversals (reason truncated to 200 chars).
- Reduced member absence threshold for raid auto-close from 15 to 5 minutes.
- Officers can now create raids (previously admin-only).
- Exclusion entries now include a reason column.
